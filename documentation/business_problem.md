# Business Problem

## Background

NovaMart Retail operates multiple stores and sells products across several categories.

Management currently does not have a consolidated view of sales performance and inventory health across stores and products. Sales performance varies between locations, while some products may have excessive inventory and others may face potential stockout risks.

The absence of a centralized analysis makes it difficult for management to identify performance gaps, monitor trends, and make informed decisions regarding sales and inventory management.

## Problem Statement

NovaMart Retail needs a data-driven analysis of its sales and inventory performance to identify sales trends, high- and low-performing products, store-level performance, and potential inventory issues.

## Business Need

Management needs a clear and interactive view of key sales and inventory metrics that can help them:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Identify high- and low-performing stores
- Understand product and category performance
- Identify potential stockout risks
- Identify excess inventory
- Understand sales trends
- Make informed sales and inventory decisions

## Expected Outcome

The project will provide an Excel-based analysis and dashboard containing key performance indicators, trends, business insights, and actionable recommendations for management.
