# KPI Definitions

## Total Revenue

Total monetary value generated from sales.

**Formula:**

Total Revenue = Sum of Sales Amount

---

## Total Profit

Total profit generated from sales after considering product costs.

**Formula:**

Total Profit = Sum of Profit

---

## Total Orders

Number of unique orders recorded.

---

## Units Sold

Total number of units sold.

**Formula:**

Units Sold = Sum of Quantity

---

## Average Order Value

Average revenue generated per order.

**Formula:**

Average Order Value = Total Revenue / Total Orders

---

## Inventory Value

Estimated monetary value of the current inventory.

**Formula:**

Inventory Value = Current Stock × Unit Cost

---

## Low-Stock Products

Number of products where current stock is below the defined reorder level.

**Business Rule:**

Current Stock < Reorder Level
